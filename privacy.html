POLARIS Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 28, 2026  |  Last updated: May 28, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Lodestone Consulting ("we," "us," or "our") collects and uses information in connection with the POLARIS (PIT Observation, Logging And Reporting Information System) operational alert program. POLARIS is a fisheries data collection and reporting system that monitors PIT-tagged fish detection sites in the Columbia River estuary.

1. Scope of this program

The POLARIS SMS alert program is an internal, staff-only system. Mobile numbers are enrolled by Lodestone Consulting for project staff and designated technical personnel only. POLARIS does not solicit or accept enrollment from the general public.

2. Information we collect

To operate the SMS alert program, we collect and retain:

  • Mobile phone number of the enrolled staff member
  • Name and role of the enrolled staff member
  • Alert subscription preferences (which sites or alert types)
  • Delivery logs (timestamps, success/failure, carrier responses)

3. How we use this information

Mobile numbers are used solely to deliver operational alerts related to POLARIS data collection. Alerts include site downtime notifications, data ingest failures, hardware errors, and other system-health events relevant to maintaining the fisheries detection network.

4. Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on system conditions. Under normal operating conditions, recipients typically receive between 0 and 10 messages per week. During incidents (e.g., multiple sites offline, network outages, hardware failures), message volume may temporarily exceed this range. Recipients also receive a daily summary message when subscribed to the daily report.

Message and data rates may apply. Standard message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply to each SMS message sent or received under this program. Consult your wireless carrier's plan for details. Lodestone Consulting is not responsible for any carrier charges incurred.

5. No sharing of mobile numbers

We do not sell, rent, lease, share, or otherwise disclose mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in information to any third party for marketing, promotional, or any other purpose. Mobile numbers and the associated opt-in data are used exclusively within Lodestone Consulting operations for the purpose of delivering POLARIS operational alerts.

The only exception is the SMS gateway service provider used to physically transmit messages (for example, an SMS API provider). The service provider receives the mobile number solely to deliver the message and is contractually prohibited from using it for any other purpose. No marketing affiliate, data broker, or unrelated third party ever receives this information.

6. Opting out

Recipients may opt out of POLARIS SMS alerts at any time by replying STOP to any alert message, or by contacting the program administrator (see Section 9). Reply HELP to any message to receive support information.

7. Data retention

Mobile numbers and delivery logs are retained for the duration of the recipient's enrollment plus a reasonable operational records retention period. Recipients may request earlier deletion by contacting the program administrator.

8. Security

Mobile numbers and associated records are stored on POLARIS infrastructure with access restricted to authorized administrators. The POLARIS server is protected by full-disk encryption and accessed via a private VPN.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, opt-out requests, or data deletion requests should be directed to the POLARIS program administrator at Lodestone Consulting.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when changes were made. Material changes affecting enrolled recipients will be communicated by SMS or other direct notice.
