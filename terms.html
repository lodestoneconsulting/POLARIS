1. Program description

POLARIS (PIT Observation, Logging And Reporting Information System) is a fisheries data collection and reporting system that monitors PIT-tagged fish detection sites in the Columbia River estuary. The POLARIS SMS alert program delivers operational notifications — such as site downtime, data ingest failures, and hardware errors — to authorized project staff and technical personnel.

2. Eligibility

Enrollment is restricted to Lodestone Consulting personnel and other technical contacts authorized by the POLARIS program administrator. The program is not open to the general public.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on system conditions. Under normal operating conditions, recipients typically receive between 0 and 10 messages per week. During incidents, message volume may temporarily increase. Recipients enrolled in the daily summary receive one additional message per day.

Message and data rates may apply. Standard SMS and data rates from your mobile carrier apply to all messages sent and received under this program. Charges are determined by your carrier plan and are the responsibility of the recipient.

5. Opting out

Recipients may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any alert message. After replying STOP, the recipient will receive one confirmation message and no further alerts. Recipients may also contact the program administrator directly to be removed.

6. Help

Reply HELP to any POLARIS alert message to receive support information. Alternatively, recipients may contact the POLARIS program administrator at Lodestone Consulting for assistance.
